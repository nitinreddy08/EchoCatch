# Code of conduct

EchoCatch is a community project that people of every background should feel welcome to join.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Be honest about what you tested and what you only believe. Wrong results are useful when reported honestly.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's personal information, and never share recordings of people without their clear consent.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, deliberate disruption, or publishing others' private information.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close or lock threads, and restrict participation for behaviour that breaks this code. To report a problem, contact the maintainer, @nitinreddy08, through GitHub (for example with a private message or a private security advisory on the repository). Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
