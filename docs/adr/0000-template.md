# ADR-NNNN: Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXXX | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who decided
- **Evidence:** spike results or benchmarks this rests on (or "none yet")

## Context

What problem forces a decision? What constraints apply (budgets, licences, platform facts from `docs/plan/02-platform-facts.md`)?

## Options considered

1. Option A: pros, cons.
2. Option B: pros, cons.

## Decision

What we chose, stated plainly.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we must now do or watch. Include how we will know the decision was wrong.

## Notes

Rules for ADRs in this repo: one decision per file; number sequentially; never edit an `Accepted` ADR's decision. Supersede it with a new ADR instead. Status, evidence, and clarifications may be updated.
